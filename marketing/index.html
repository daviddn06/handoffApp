How it works

Three steps, either direction.

Handoff makes the introduction. You handle the lease and payments directly.

  1. Step 01

    Pick your campus and your dates.

    Filter by budget, rooms, and the months you actually need. Listing instead? Add your rent, dates, and photos.

  2. Step 02

    Compare them on the map.

    Price and the walk to campus in the same view, so a good deal three blocks further out is easy to spot.

  3. Step 03

    Send a request, then talk.

    Your email stays private until the listing owner accepts. After that you both see it and take it from there.

  • Students only Sign up with the email that matches your campus and verify it once.
  • Free, both ways Browsing, listing, and requests cost nothing. Handoff never touches rent or deposits.
  • Private until accepted Contact details are revealed to both students only once a request is accepted.
