Take a look around
How does student verification work?

Choose your campus and sign up with its matching school email address. We email you a six digit code and a link, either one works. You can browse right away and verify when you want to list a place or send a handoff request.

Verification confirms access to that email. It does not verify a person's identity, enrollment, property, or lease.

Why is a sublease cheaper than the lease?

The student leaving is already on the hook for the rent. Any month they recover is money back, so most subleases are priced under the original lease. Both sides end up better off than an empty room.

Is Handoff free to use?

Yes. Browsing, posting listings, and sending requests are free. Handoff does not collect rent or deposits. You and the other student arrange the lease and any payments directly.

When is my email shared?

Your email is not shown on public listings or pending requests. When a listing owner accepts a request, both students can see each other's email in the app. Keep contact details out of your listing and introduction if you want them to stay private until then.

Can I list a room in a shared apartment?

Yes. Choose a private room, shared room, or entire place. Include clear photos, accurate dates, and the details someone would want to know about roommates, shared spaces, utilities, and lease requirements.

Which campuses are supported?

You can sign up and browse at USC, UCLA, LMU, and CSULB. Listings are added by students, so availability varies by campus. If yours is quiet, your place could be the first.

What should we handle before moving in?

Arrange a visit, check the property and lease details, and confirm any required landlord approval. Agree on the terms directly. Handoff connects students and does not inspect properties, sign leases, process payments, or resolve lease disputes.
